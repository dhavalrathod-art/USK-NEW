# Mobile App – Scholarship View Details (content from the web portal)

Opens when the student taps **See Eligibility** on a card of "Scholarship Main Page".
One screen per scholarship: **CMSS** and **MYSY**. Text is copied from the web portal (`index.html` → `SCH_LIST`, `SCH_CRIT`).

**Header:** ← back, title "Scholarship Details" (navy header, same as the other screens).

**Card layout (same style as "Scholarship List" card):** white card, 19px radius, light grey border, 15px padding, 10px gap.
Order: title (Inter Medium 14) → department (Inter Medium 11, grey) → gold tag "Benefit" → benefit text (italic 11) → blue info box (application period + "Check your eligibility and keep all documents ready before applying.") → section "Eligibility checks" (navy dash list) → second section (navy dash list) → navy button **Apply Scholarship Now** (opens the web portal).

## CMSS

- **Title:** Chief Minister Scholarship Scheme (CMSS)
- **Department:** Education Department, Government of Gujarat
- **Benefit:** Tuition fee reimbursement for Diploma / UG courses (MYSY assistance, if any, is adjusted).
- **Application period:** 01/10/2026 – 15/12/2026

**Eligibility checks**
1. Special category – At least one of the 7 categories below applies to you.
2. Education pathway – Class 10 → Diploma, Diploma → UG, or Class 12 Science / General → UG.
3. Family income – Annual family income from all sources ₹4,50,000 or less.
4. Domicile – Gujarat domicile.

**Special categories and supporting documents**
1. Category 1 – Girl who passed from a school / college in a taluka where female literacy is below 50% (current KCG taluka list). *Documents: School / College Taluka Proof (to be confirmed)*
2. Category 2 – Child of Army / para-military / Reserve Police / Gujarat Police personnel who died on duty (war, terrorism, Naxalism) or became permanently disabled on duty. *Documents: Competent Authority Certificate*
3. Category 3 – Child of an organised-sector worker, salt worker, silicosis-affected worker, or a landless unorganised worker who died in an accident (not construction workers). *Documents: As per worker type – Bonafide + Worker ID, Family Identity Card, Medical + Labour Officer certificates, or Labour Officer inquiry report*
4. Category 4 – Student, father, mother or guardian with a disability of 40% or more. *Documents: UDID Card or Disability Certificate*
5. Category 5 – Child of a widow mother who has not remarried. *Documents: Father's Death Certificate, Widow Certificate and Non-Remarriage Affidavit*
6. Category 6 – Orphan – both parents have died (not adopted). *Documents: Parents' Death Certificates, Orphanage Certificate or Orphan Certificate; Guardian Affidavit if living with a relative*
7. Category 7 – Child of a divorced or abandoned (Tyakta) mother who has not remarried. *Documents: Divorce proof + Non-Remarriage Affidavit, or Mamlatdar / Gazetted Officer certificate or Abandonment Affidavit*

Note for the app: the student picks the category on the **web portal** when applying, not in the app (list is read-only here).

## MYSY

- **Title:** Mukhyamantri Yuva Swavalamban Yojana (MYSY)
- **Department:** Education Department, Government of Gujarat
- **Benefit:** Tuition fee, hostel and book assistance as per the MYSY rules.
- **Application period:** 01/10/2026 – 30/11/2026

**Eligibility checks**
1. Admission pathway – UG after Class 12 Science / General or Diploma; Diploma after Class 10.
2. Eligible course – Course is in the MYSY eligible course list.
3. MBBS – MBBS admission must be through NEET.
4. Seat type – Not admitted on an NRI seat.
5. Previous marks – Class 10 / Class 12: 80 percentile or more. Diploma: 65% or more.
6. Family income – Annual family income ₹6,00,000 or less.
7. Domicile – Gujarat domicile.

**Special cases**
1. Main earning member of the family died: considered for MYSY for the remaining period of the course.
2. Child of a Gujarat-domicile Army / para-military jawan who died on duty (war, terrorism, Naxalism): marks and income limits are not applied.
3. Integrated course of up to 6 years after Class 10: first 3 years as Class 10 → Diploma, last 3 years as Diploma → Degree.
4. College outside Gujarat: eligible for MYSY.

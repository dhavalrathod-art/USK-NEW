992px and above · ExpandedSidebar is always visible at 260px with the logo, organization name and menu labels. Page content starts after the sidebar.--sidebar-w: 260px
992px and above · CollapsedThe toggle shrinks the sidebar to 80px: logo and icons only. Hovering an icon shows its name in a tooltip..app.collapsed · --sidebar-mini: 80px
Below 992px · OverlaySidebar is hidden. The toggle slides it in from the left with the logo and organization name, over a dark backdrop. The page behind does not scroll..app.mobile-open
Sidebar rules (below 992px)
  • The sidebar is hidden by default. Only the toggle button in the header opens it.
  • The open sidebar always shows the logo + organization name at the top, then the full menu with labels, then Log out.
  • It closes on a backdrop click, a menu item click, the Esc key, or when the screen becomes 992px or wider.
  • The collapsed (80px) mode is for 992px and above only. It is never used on tablet or mobile.
03

Components on Small Screens

What changes in each part of the page when the screen gets smaller.

Header
  • Height stays 64px. Toggle button is always on the left.
  • Below 576px the "Academic Year" line is hidden; the page title stays.
  • Notification, settings and profile icons stay; spacing becomes smaller.
Page & Cards
  • Page padding: 20px on desktop, 14px below 576px.
  • Cards stack one below the other. Never place 2 cards side by side below 768px.
  • The page header title and buttons wrap to two lines when there is no space.
  • The page must never scroll sideways. Only tables may scroll inside their card.
Forms & Filters
  • Form grid: 4 columns on desktop → 2 on tablet → 1 on mobile.
  • Labels always stay above the field.
  • On mobile, footer buttons (Cancel, Submit) become full width; Primary stays last.
  • The Advanced Filter panel stays collapsed by default on mobile.
Tables
  • The table keeps its columns and scrolls sideways inside its card.
  • The Action column stays fixed (sticky) on the right.
  • Toolbar, bulk bar and pagination wrap to the next line on small screens.
  • Hide only less important columns when needed; never hide Name, Status or Action.
Modal & Drawer
  • Below 560px a modal opens from the bottom at full width (bottom sheet).
  • Header and footer stay fixed; only the body scrolls.
  • The detail drawer takes up to 92% of the screen width.
Touch & Text
  • Every button and icon that can be tapped is at least 40 × 40px.
  • Keep at least 8px between two tap targets.
  • Smallest text is 11px. Don't make text smaller on mobile.
  • Hover-only information (like tooltips) must also be reachable by tap.

  • No sideways scroll on the page; no text cut off or overlapping.

  • Long Gujarati text still fits in buttons, labels and table headers.
